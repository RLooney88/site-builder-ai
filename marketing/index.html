Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

Absolutely not. That's the whole point. You just tell Themis what you want in plain English, and it handles all the technical stuff. If you can send a text message, you can update your website.

What if the AI makes a mistake?

You always preview changes before they go live. Nothing hits your website until you approve it. If something doesn't look right, just ask for a revision or cancel the change.

Can I use my existing website?

Yes! We can migrate your current site to Themis. Whether it's WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, or custom-built, we'll get it set up and ready to edit with AI.

What kind of websites do you support?

We work with any static website—business sites, portfolios, landing pages, blogs, and more. If it's on the web, we can help you manage it.

How fast are changes?

Most changes are ready to preview in under a minute. Once you approve, they're live immediately. No more waiting days for your developer to get back to you.
